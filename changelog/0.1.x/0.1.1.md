# 0.1.1 — 2026-09-30

## Added

- **`artic_search_artworks`** — collection search by text and filters (artist, department, type, style, subject, classification, place of origin, gallery, year range, public domain, on view, has image), with facet counts, date sorting, and `limit: 0` for counts only.
- **`artic_get_artworks`** — full records for up to 10 ids: opt-in text `sections` (description, provenance, exhibition and publication history, catalogue), IIIF image URLs marked `public_domain` or `in_copyright`, and related multimedia; `deferred_ids` names records held back by the response budget.
- **`artic_search_artists`** — find artists, cultures, and organizations by name, or fetch up to 25 by id, with life dates, alternate names, the museum's artwork count, and up to three sample works.
- **`artic_search_exhibitions`** — past, current, and upcoming exhibitions by text, `when`, and an overlapping date window, with dates, gallery, summary, web page, and the artworks and artist ids the museum lists.
- **`artic_search_audio_guide`** — text search over audio-guide stop titles and transcripts, returning each stop's MP3 URL and transcript with the license text and a `source_citation`.
- **`artic_lookup_vocabulary`** — values of ten collection vocabularies with artwork counts, narrowed by `contains`; `filter_param` names the `artic_search_artworks` filter that accepts them as listed.
- **`artic://artworks/{id}`** — one artwork record as JSON: the `artic_get_artworks` record with description, provenance, and related media, plus its license text and attribution.
- **`AicService`** — one client for `api.artic.edu`, paced under the 60-per-minute anonymous limit, retried inside a 20 s deadline, cached in process, and identified by an `AIC-User-Agent` header; failures map to `rate_limited`, `request_blocked`, and `upstream_rejected_query`.
- **Config** — `AIC_CONTACT` (printable ASCII, defaults to the repository URL) names the contact in `AIC-User-Agent`; `AIC_REQUESTS_PER_MINUTE` (1-600, default 50) sets the outbound budget.
- **Data terms** — artwork, artist, exhibition, and audio-guide results carry the API's `license_text`: metadata is CC0, artwork descriptions are CC BY 4.0 with `description_attribution`, images are reusable only when `is_public_domain`, and audio-guide content is for noncommercial educational use.
